Privacy Policy – My Chinchilla (first game)

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy covers the first My Chinchilla game for Android (package com.PlejaderGames.MyChinchilla). The game shows rewarded video ads, offers in-app purchases and has online features: a player account, rankings, friends, messages, auctions, gifts and visiting other players' chinchillas.

Data controller

The data controller is Jan Nalepka, an individual publishing the game as Plejader Games.

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

We do not collect your real name, email address, location, contacts or payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Your player name, profile, chinchillas and ranking scores are visible to other players. Do not use your real name as your player name and do not share personal details in messages.

Screenshots from the game go only to your phone's gallery or to an app you choose when sharing. We do not upload them to our server.

Chinchilla notifications are scheduled locally on the phone, only with your permission. You can withdraw it in your system settings.

Ads

The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are provided by Unity Ads. Unity may use your device's advertising ID to select ads and measure their performance. You can turn off personalised ads by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads). Unity's privacy policy explains more and how to opt out.

Google and Unity servers may be located outside the European Economic Area, including the USA. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Retention and deletion

  • Progress saved on the phone is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Your account, profile, messages and server backup are kept until you ask us to delete them or until the game server is shut down.
  • The game is being gradually retired. When the server is shut down, we will delete the player data stored on it.

To delete your account and server data, email plejadergames@gmail.com with the subject "My Chinchilla data deletion" and include your player name. We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Just write to the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl).

Age

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe such data has reached us, write to us and we will delete it promptly.

The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the last change.
