Privacy policy – Gerbil Calculator

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Gerbil Calculator does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools and does not require an account.

Publisher

Gerbil Calculator (Android, package com.plejaderworks.gerbilcalculator) is published by Jan Nalepka, a private individual, under the name Plejader Games.

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

How the app works

All genetic calculations run entirely on your phone. The genes you choose for the female and the male never leave the device. The app requests no permissions – it has no access to the internet, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.

Data stored on the device

DataPurposeWhere
Number of calculations performedShowing a one-time request to rate the app after the 5th calculationOn the phone only
Currently selected genesShowing the calculation resultsOn the phone only, while the app is running

This data is not sent to us and cannot identify anyone. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

The app uses Android Auto Backup. Android may on its own include the calculation counter in a backup on your Google account and restore it when you install the app on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google and can be turned off in your phone's backup settings.

The rating dialog is handled entirely by the Google Play Store app on your phone. Any rating and review you leave goes directly to Google and is published on Google Play under Google's rules, linked to your Google account. The app does not tell us whether or how you rated it. Google decides whether the dialog appears.

The Manrope font is provided by Google Play services installed on your phone, which download it from Google's servers when needed. The app does not pass any information about you or your calculations in the process.

Downloading the app from Google Play is subject to Google's terms. Google may share aggregated, anonymous statistics with us (for example install counts, or crash reports if you agreed to send them in your Android settings).

Your rights

Because we do not process your personal data, we hold no data that we could give you, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page before releasing that version. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the last change.
