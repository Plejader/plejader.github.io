Gerbil Calculator

A gerbil colour genetics calculator for Android. Pick the genes of the female and the male, and the app shows which colours the pups can have and how likely each one is.

  • Genes: agouti, spot, colorpoint, umbrous, dilute, pink eye, extension and underwhite
  • Warnings about lethal combinations
  • A description of each colour
  • Works offline, no ads, no account needed
