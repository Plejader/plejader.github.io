Privacy policy – My Animals

Last updated: 26 November 2026

My Animals is an app for keeping breeding records. Everything you enter is stored on your phone. We do not collect it and have no access to it. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools and does not require an account.

Publisher

My Animals (Android, package com.plejaderworks.breederanimalfamily) is published by Jan Nalepka, an individual, under the name Plejader Games.

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

Google Drive backup (Premium)

This feature only runs when you start it yourself and sign in to your Google account.

  • Sign-in is handled by Google (Google Sign-In). The app receives basic account details (email address, name, profile picture) and only displays the email address so you can see which account the backup goes to. We do not store these details or send them anywhere else.
  • The app requests the drive.file scope, which gives access only to files the app created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • A backup is a JSON file containing all your animal records, pairings and photos, saved in a folder called “BreederAnimalFamily Backups” in your Google Drive. The file goes straight from your phone to your Drive. It does not pass through our servers and we have no access to it.
  • You can list, restore and delete backups in the app, or delete them directly in Google Drive.
  • You can sign out in the app, and you can revoke the app's access to your account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Android system backup

The app uses Android Auto Backup. If backup is turned on on your phone, Android may save the app's data (including the animal database with photos) to a backup in your Google account and restore it when you install the app on a new phone. This backup is run by Google, we have no access to it, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or other payment details. Google shares order information with us (e.g. date, amount and country) under its own terms. The app only checks with Google Play whether Premium has been purchased.

Downloading the app from Google Play is subject to Google's terms. Google may share aggregated, anonymous statistics with us (e.g. install counts, or crash reports if you agreed to send them in your Android settings).

Your rights

You are in full control of your animal records: you can edit and delete them in the app, and remove all of them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. You can delete Google Drive backups in the app or in Drive. We do not keep your personal data ourselves, so we have nothing to provide, correct or delete. The exception is Google Play order data; you can contact us about it at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page before releasing that version. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the latest change.
