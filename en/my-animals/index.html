My Animals

A breeder's app for Android. Keep track of your animals, their pedigrees, pairings and litters in one place.

  • A profile for every animal: photo, date of birth, variety, parents and notes
  • Parents and offspring of every animal
  • Pairings, births and litter summaries
  • Health history and weight chart
  • Backup to your own Google Drive (Premium)
  • No ads, no account needed, your data stays on your phone
