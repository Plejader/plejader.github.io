Privacy policy – Chinchilla Calculator

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Chinchilla Calculator does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools and does not require an account.

Publisher

Chinchilla Calculator (Android, package com.plejaderworks.chinchillacalculator) is published by Jan Nalepka, an individual, under the name Plejader Games.

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

How the app works

All genetic calculations run entirely on your phone. The genes you choose for the female and the male never leave the device. The app requests no permissions – it has no access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. The chinchilla photos shown in the colour descriptions are built into the app.

Data stored on the device

DataPurposeWhere
Number of calculations made and whether a rating request is dueShowing a request to rate the app after 5, 20 and 50 calculationsOn the phone only
Currently selected genesKeeping your selection, e.g. when the screen rotatesOn the phone only, while the app is running

This data is never sent to us and cannot identify anyone. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone settings.

The app uses Android Auto Backup. Android may back up the calculation counter to your Google account and restore it when you install the app on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google and can be turned off in your phone's backup settings.

Your rights

Because we do not process your personal data, we hold no data to give you access to, correct or delete. If you have any questions, write to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page before releasing that version. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the last change.
