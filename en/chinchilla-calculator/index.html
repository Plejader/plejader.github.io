Chinchilla Calculator

A chinchilla colour genetics calculator for Android. Pick the genes of the female and the male, and the app shows which colours the kits can have and how likely each one is.

  • Genes: white (Wilson), beige, black velvet, ebony, violet, sapphire, goldbar and charcoal
  • Warnings about lethal combinations
  • A description of each colour, with photos
  • Works offline, no ads, no account needed
