Privacy Policy – My Chinchilla

Last updated: November 25, 2026

My Chinchilla shows no ads and does not track players. It collects only the data needed to back up game progress and handle purchases.

Data controller

The data controller is Jan Nalepka, a private individual who publishes My Chinchilla under the name Plejader Games (Android, package com.plejadergames.mychinchilla2).

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Chinchilla notifications are scheduled locally on the phone, only with the player's consent. We do not send them from a server. Consent can be withdrawn in the system settings.

The game uses Android Auto Backup. Android may automatically save game progress in a backup on the player's Google account and restore it after installing on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google and can be turned off in the phone's backup settings.

Third-party services

We share data only with the providers the game cannot work without. We do not sell data or share it with advertisers.

These companies' servers may be located outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Data retention and deletion

  • Progress saved on the phone is removed when the game is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
  • The cloud backup and Unity account are kept until the player asks for them to be deleted.
  • Signing out in the game removes the sign-in session from the phone but does not delete the cloud backup.

To delete your account and cloud backup, email plejadergames@gmail.com with the subject “My Chinchilla data deletion”. We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm it in our reply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of and port your data. You also have the right to object. Just write to the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or with the authority in your country.

Age of players

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe such data has reached us, write to us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We will announce significant changes in the game's update notes. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the latest change.
