Privacy Policy – My Family Tree

Last updated: 26 November 2026

My Family Tree keeps your family tree on your phone. We do not run any server and we never receive the data you enter. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and does not require an account. Data leaves your device only when you choose to: by exporting a file or by creating a backup on your own Google Drive.

Publisher

My Family Tree (Android, package com.plejaderworks.myfamilytree) is published by Jan Nalepka, a private individual, under the name Plejader Games.

Privacy contact: plejadergames@gmail.com.

Data you enter into the tree

For each person in the tree you can store:

  • name and maiden name,
  • sex,
  • date of birth and date of death,
  • family relations (mother, father, partner, adoption),
  • a photo,
  • notes.

You decide which of these fields to fill in. The data is stored in the app's local database on your phone and is not sent to us. It usually concerns other people too – your relatives. You enter it for your own private use. If you share the tree with others (for example by sending an export file), remember that you are also sharing these people's data.

Google Drive backup (Premium)

After buying Premium you can sign in with your Google account and save copies of your tree to your own Google Drive.

  • Sign-in uses Google Sign-In. The app receives your email address only to show which account the backups are saved to. The app does not store the address or send it to us.
  • The app requests only the drive.file scope – it can access only the files it created itself (the "MyFamilyTree Backups" folder), not the rest of your Drive.
  • A backup is a JSON file containing the whole tree, including photos. It goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive – it does not pass through our servers and we have no access to it.
  • In the app you can browse, restore and delete backups; you can also delete them directly in Google Drive. "Sign out" disconnects your Google account from the app. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Storage of files on Google Drive is governed by Google's policies.

Exporting and importing files

You can export the tree to a JSON file (with photos) in a location you choose, import a tree from a JSON or GEDCOM file, and merge in data from a file received from a family member. Files are processed only on your phone. What happens to an exported file afterwards (for example, who you send it to) is up to you.

The app does not store your Premium purchase status – it is checked with Google Play every time the app starts.

The app uses Android Auto Backup. Android may itself save the app's data (including the tree database) in a backup on your Google account and restore it when you install the app on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google, is separate from the Premium backup, and can be turned off in your phone's backup settings.

All data stored on the phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. You can delete individual people in the app at any time.

Security

Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). Data on the phone is protected by Android's app sandbox – other apps cannot access the My Family Tree database. Exported JSON files are not encrypted, so store and share them with care.

Your rights

We do not keep your data on any server, so we hold no data that we could provide, correct or delete – you have full control over it in the app, on your phone and on your Google Drive. If you have questions, write to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect anyone's data on our servers. Children's data entered into a tree by the user stays on the user's phone (or in their Google Drive backup), like the rest of the tree.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts processing data differently, we will update this page before releasing that version. The current version is always at this address, and the date at the top shows the last change.
