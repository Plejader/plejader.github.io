My Family Tree

A family tree app for Android. Add your relatives, link them across generations, and explore the tree by panning and pinch-zooming.

  • Parents, children, partners, adoption and maiden names
  • Photos, birth and death dates, and notes
  • GEDCOM import, JSON export and import
  • Premium: backups to your own Google Drive and merging trees with family members
  • Your data stays on your phone, no ads, no account needed
